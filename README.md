# Geode

**Collectible gem cards for DevFest Sudbury attendees. Pick your gem, get your card.**

A geode looks like a plain grey rock from the outside. Crack it open and it's full of crystals.
That's the idea: every developer, designer, student and builder at DevFest has something
special inside, and Geode turns it into a card worth sharing.

Built by GDG Sudbury volunteers for **DevFest Sudbury 2026** (November 28, Collège Boréal).

---

## The story

Roughly 1.85 billion years ago, a massive meteorite impact formed the Sudbury Basin. That
impact shaped the region and helped give it the nickel, copper and mineral wealth Sudbury is
known for today.

Today Sudbury is also home to a growing community of developers, engineers, students, creators
and founders. Geode connects the two stories: the ground under Sudbury was shaped by one big
impact, and the people building here are making their own.

## How it works

1. Open the site on any phone or laptop. There's nothing to install.
2. Enter your name (and, if you like, a photo).
3. Pick the gem that matches what you build.
4. Get your card: download it, share it on LinkedIn, or keep it as your DevFest keepsake.

### What's on a card

- Your name, and your photo if you add one
- Your gem, its track and its artwork
- DevFest Sudbury 2026 · November 28 · Collège Boréal
- A collector number
- GDG Sudbury branding and event hashtags

## Privacy

Cards are generated **in your browser**. Your name and photo are not uploaded or stored on
any server, and we don't collect email addresses. Close the tab and they're gone.

Contributors: never commit real attendee data, photos of real people (other than your own,
for testing), API keys or `.env` files. This is a public repository.

## Contributing

New contributors are welcome, whether you write code, design or just want to help test.

1. Pick an open issue (or open one with your idea) and comment that you're taking it.
2. Create a branch from `main`: `feat/short-description` or `fix/short-description`.
3. Open a pull request into `main` with a short description and a screenshot for anything visual.
4. Someone on the team reviews it before it's merged.

Questions? Ask in the GDG Sudbury volunteers' chat, or open an issue.
